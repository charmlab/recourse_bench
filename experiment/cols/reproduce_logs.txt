**Scope of Reproducibility**

We aim to reproduce the COLS claims from `reference/cols.pdf`:

1. We generate sets of recourse actions that improve expected user satisfaction under cost uncertainty : [Quantitative] : [Tables 1 and 2]
2. We improve coverage, diversity, proximity, sparsity, validity, and EMC-style satisfaction metrics over baselines : [Quantitative] : [Tables 1-3]
3. We provide monotonic optimization guarantees for the proposed objective : [Qualitative] : [Theorem / proof section]
4. We analyze fairness and user-facing recourse quality : [Quantitative] : [Fairness summaries and human-evaluation discussion]

**Methodology**

We reproduce COLS with `experiment/cols/test_cols_reproduce.py` and `experiment/cols/reproduce_configs.yaml`. Code/experiments and data are available. The current script targets Tables 1-3 in the RecourseBench environment.

**Results**

Claims 1, 2, and the simulated part of Claim 4 are partially reproduced: 59 of 70 scalar comparisons are within the 15% relative-delta threshold. Important discrepancies remain, including LS Diversity FS@1, reference 0.0 versus reproduced 0.3846, and Adult Binary COLS PAC, reference 0.38 versus reproduced 0.1695. Claim 3 is proof-level evidence, and human-evaluation claims are not fully reproduced by simulated metrics.
