Reproducibility Criterion
- A result is treated as one scalar `(dataset, model, metric)` value.
- The symmetric relative error is `Δ(m, m̂) = |m̂ - m| / max(|m|, |m̂|, ε0)`.
- Numerical-stability constant: `ε0 = 1e-12`.
- Fixed reproduction tolerance: `δ = 0.15`.
