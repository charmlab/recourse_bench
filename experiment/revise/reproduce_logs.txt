**Scope of Reproducibility**

We aim to reproduce the REVISE claims from `reference/revise.pdf`:

1. We generate realistic individual recourse by optimizing in the latent space of a generative model : [Quantitative] : [Credit and tabular recourse experiments]
2. We compare latent-manifold recourse against input-space or baseline counterfactual methods : [Quantitative] : [Quantitative tables and examples]
3. We analyze linear/MLP classifiers and causal-system examples for actionability and realism : [Quantitative] : [MLP/linear comparisons and causal experiments]

**Methodology**

We run two levels of coverage:

1. `experiment/revise/smoke.py` with `experiment/revise/smoke_config.yaml`, which remains a small Credit + linear-model executability check with short VAE training.
2. `experiment/revise/test_revise_reproduce.py`, a deterministic scoped manifold check anchored to Algorithm 1 and Section 3 latent-space optimization over a generative model. The script compares direct input-space optimization against latent-space optimization through a known decoder `x = [z, z^2]` and writes `experiment/revise/reproduction_report.json`.

The project artifact list marks REVISE as having no original code/experiments and no original data, so exact same-tools reproduction is blocked. The new script is not the original credit, MLP/linear, or causal-system experiment.

**Results**

Claim 1 is partially validated at mechanism level. The latent-space counterfactual achieved target probability 0.9925 and zero manifold residual under the known decoder, showing that optimization through a generative model can produce valid recourse on the modeled data manifold.

Claim 2 is partially validated in a scoped synthetic comparison. Direct input-space optimization also achieved high target probability, 0.9940, but had manifold residual 0.3443. Latent-space optimization had comparable distance, 1.4407 versus 1.4392, while staying exactly on the manifold. This validates the trend behind latent-manifold recourse but does not reproduce the paper's quantitative tables or baseline suite.

Claim 3 is not reproduced. The scoped script does not analyze the paper's linear/MLP classifier comparisons or causal-system examples.
