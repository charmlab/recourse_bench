**Methodology**

We reproduce TreX with `experiment/trex/test_trex_reproduce.py` and `experiment/trex/config.yaml`. Code/experiments and data are available. The current run covers German, HELOC, and CTG with L1/L2 settings in the RecourseBench environment.

**Results**

Claims 2 and 3 are partially reproduced: 16 of 24 scalar comparisons are within the 15% relative-delta threshold. Future-validity values are often close, including HELOC L2 leave-one-out validity, reference 98.2 versus reproduced 99.10, relative delta 0.009. Cost and LOF drift more: German L1 cost has relative delta 0.324, HELOC L1 LOF has relative delta 1.731, and CTG L1 LOF has relative delta 1.509. Claim 1 is proof-level evidence.
