**Methodology**

**Results**

Claims 1 and 3 are largely reproduced for Table 1: 18 of 20 scalar comparisons are within the 15% relative-delta threshold. COMPAS L1 is reference 0.039 versus reproduced 0.0357, relative delta 0.084, and COMPAS LOF is reference 1.24 versus reproduced 1.1548, relative delta 0.069. The main discrepancies are Adult L1, relative delta 0.342, and HELOC L1, relative delta 0.227. Claim 2 is proof-level evidence.
