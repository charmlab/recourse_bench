**Scope of Reproducibility**

We aim to reproduce the CFVAE claims from `reference/cfvae.pdf`:

1. We generate counterfactuals with a conditional variational autoencoder under user-specified causal/actionability constraints : [Quantitative] : [Adult constraint-feasibility experiments]
2. We satisfy constraints more reliably than baseline VAE, model-approximation, and example-based methods : [Quantitative] : [Adult age and age+education tables]
3. We preserve useful proximity while maintaining target-class validity : [Quantitative] : [Validity, feasibility, and proximity metrics]
4. We use amortized generation rather than per-instance optimization : [Quantitative] : [Methodology and timing/amortization discussion]

**Methodology**

We reproduce CFVAE with `experiment/cfvae/test_cfvae_reproduce.py` and the available CFVAE weights/artifacts. Code/experiments, data, and pretrained artifacts are available. The current run covers Adult age and age+education constraints across BaseCVAE, BaseVAE, ModelApprox, and ExampleBased methods in the RecourseBench environment.

**Results**

Claims 1-3 are reproduced for the covered Adult constraint settings. All 32 scalar comparisons are within the 15% relative-delta threshold, usually within 1%. The largest observed deltas are small, including Adult age+education BaseVAE constraint feasibility, reference 42.5929 versus reproduced 42.9647, relative delta 0.009. Claim 4 is only partially reproduced because the report emphasizes feasibility/proximity rather than a full generation-time comparison.
